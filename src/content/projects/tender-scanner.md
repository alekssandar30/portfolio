TenderScanner is a public procurement technology product for companies working with tender opportunities across Serbia and Bosnia and Herzegovina.

The product brings together a React and TypeScript interface, backend services, PostgreSQL-backed data processing and AI-assisted analysis in a production SaaS platform.
