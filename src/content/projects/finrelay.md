Finrelay builds white-label payments infrastructure for fintechs - payment orchestration, acquiring, and core banking for electronic money institutions. My eight months there were spent on the frontend of the merchant and back-office UI: the screens where payments, merchants and operations actually get managed.

The largest piece was a full redesign of the platform. I introduced a new design system built on Angular and PrimeNG and carried the existing screens over to it - the kind of change that touches everything at once and has to land without disrupting a product operating in a regulated payments domain.
